MINEIT FIELD MANUAL

MineIT is a persistent mining-corporation game. There is no final stop after Contract 01: build profitable colonies, improve permanent corporation technology and expand to increasingly difficult worlds.

{{INDEX_ITEMS}}

1. Objective & game loop

Accept a colony charter, survey the world, develop useful resource sites, keep the population supplied, expand housing and Industry, trade surplus stock for cash and reach the contract's Food Production, Industry and Population goals. Contract profit is local trade revenue minus that colony's tracked costs.

Your corporation's cash and Power/Food/Mining technology are global. Colony maps, population, inventories, infrastructure, consumption, operating costs, contract age and survey queues are local to each colony.

After the first successful 10-year charter you can keep that colony and open additional contracts. The long-term game is managing an expanding portfolio rather than replacing one world with the next.

2. Screen, time & controls

Top: contract progress and the current colony's Food Production, Industry and Population objectives.

Map: drag to pan, tap an unsurveyed tile to queue a survey, tap a revealed tile for full details, or hold and drag across unsurveyed tiles to multi-select a survey queue.

Footer stock: Cash is corporation cash; Stock Value includes quality-adjusted sale value; Food, Build, Fuel and Ore are the current colony's stored totals.

Time: Pause, ×1, ×2 and ×4 change simulation speed. A docked corporate trade ship pauses positive time speeds until it departs.

COLLECT: current active extraction sites. SHIP: next visit or docked trade. COLONY: local infrastructure and needs. COLONIES: portfolio switcher. TECH: permanent corporation licences. GOALS: contract performance. ☰: save, diagnostics and this manual.

3. Surveying & discovery

Unsurveyed sectors show ?. Surveys take game days; distance from the ship and the world's scan difficulty increase the duration. Mining technology gradually reduces scan time.

Mining technology also increases simultaneous survey slots from 1 up to 5: approximately one extra slot every two Mining levels. Higher Mining levels improve scanner hints as well.

A tap queues one sector. Hold + drag selects many sectors and adds them to the queue. When a slot becomes free, the next queued sector starts automatically. Completed surveys reveal resource type, rarity, exact quality, deposit/abundance size and the Mining technology required to exploit it.

Surveying stops when a colony's mining contract has ended and it becomes support-only.

4. Map tiles & visibility filters

Revealed resource tiles show the resource icon, exact Q value and deposit size/renewable abundance. Developed tiles also show their site level and depletion bar. Technology-blocked resources are ghosted but remain tappable for their details and Mining requirement.

The map has three independent filter groups. State controls Developed, Not Developed and Locked tiles. Size controls Limited, Established, Small, Modest, Large, Huge, Colossal, Vast and legacy deposits. Quality uses the six economic quality bands. Every button is multi-select: active means visible. A revealed tile must pass all three groups to be drawn.

Unsurveyed sectors and the ship are never removed by resource filters, so filtering cannot prevent exploration. Filtered revealed tiles become blank grid cells and cannot be opened accidentally until their filter is re-enabled.

6. Quality, production & sale value

Every natural deposit has an exact quality from Q1 to Q10,000. Quality has two effects. First, extraction output increases using a curved base-output formula (8 + 7 × Q^0.52) before resource, abundance, site-level and technology multipliers. Second, harvested stock is placed into one of six quality-price bands:

{{QUALITY_ROWS}}

Inventory stays collapsed to one top-level resource quantity, but its quality-band quantities are preserved underneath. The corporate ship prices each band separately. Colony consumption deliberately uses the cheapest available Food, Fuel or Ore grades first so high-value stock is preserved where possible.

Resources bought from the corporation enter storage as Excellent / ×1.00 grade.

7. Deposit size, renewables & depletion

Finite resources are generated as Small, Modest, Large, Huge or Colossal deposits. The class describes the original generated deposit; the numeric amount on the tile is its original total reserve. Extraction reduces the remaining reserve until it reaches zero, at which point the site becomes depleted and stops producing.

Renewable resources use abundance classes Limited, Established, Large or Vast and display ∞ instead of a finite reserve. They do not deplete. Abundance directly affects their baseline production rate.

The progress bar on a developed finite tile shows remaining reserve as a proportion of its original deposit. The tile details show current remaining stock and estimated life at the current production rate.

8. Developing & upgrading resource sites

Surveying only discovers a resource. To extract it, the required Mining technology must be owned and the colony must pay both corporation cash and Build material.

Development cost rises with resource quality, distance from the ship and world difficulty. Cash is approximately (1,800 + √Q × 70) × (1 + distance × 0.018) × world cost. Build cost is approximately (28 + √Q × 0.42 + distance × 1.2) × world cost.

A developed site starts at L1 and collects automatically every game day. Each site level after L1 adds roughly 22% to extraction. Upgrades become progressively more expensive: cash grows by about ×1.68 per existing level and Build by ×1.38.

Higher Food technology multiplies Food-site output; higher Mining technology multiplies Build, Fuel and Ore extraction. Ended mining contracts disable new development, upgrades and extraction.

9. Colony economy, power & survival

Food: daily demand is about 0.055 per colonist. Shortages reduce colony stability and severe shortages can shrink population. Stable Food and Power allow gradual growth until housing or Power-tech population capacity is reached.

Power: demand is population ×0.08 + Industry level ×8 + active sites ×1.5. Power technology supplies a capacity limit and a fuel-intensity value. Fuel demand is power demand × fuel intensity. If generation capacity or Fuel is short, the Power factor drops.

Industry: each Industry level consumes about 0.75 Ore/day. Effective Industry capability is Industry level ×100, reduced by whichever is worse: available Power or Ore supply. Power technology also caps the maximum Industry level.

Build: not passively consumed. It is spent on site development/upgrades, housing and Industry expansion. Construction consumption also prefers cheaper-quality Build grades first if several grades exist.

Housing: each expansion adds 160 capacity. Cash and Build costs rise with housing level, and Power technology imposes a population-support ceiling.

Operating cash cost: population, Industry, housing and active sites all add daily cost. Harder colony tiers increase that cost by 20% per tier above Tier 1. Holdover multiplies costs by ×{{HOLDOVER_COST_MULTIPLIER}}; liability status multiplies them by ×{{LIABILITY_COST_MULTIPLIER}}.

Synthetic Food: Food technology can manufacture food. Worlds without natural food receive the full synthetic output; worlds with natural food receive 35% of that figure as supplemental production.

10. Corporation technology

Power, Food Production and Mining each have 10 permanent levels. Licences belong to the corporation, so every colony benefits from them. You may only buy the immediate next level; tiers cannot be skipped.

Some contracts have a direct corporate technology link. Deep-reach contracts are ship-only, so technology can only be purchased while the corporate vessel is docked.

Hide Future Tech hides levels beyond the next purchasable tier, but always leaves the current and next tier visible. Hide Old Tech hides already purchased levels below the current tier. The two controls can be combined.

11. Corporate trade ship

The first corporate ship is due on game day {{FIRST_TRADE_DAY}}; later visits are every {{TRADE_INTERVAL_DAYS}} days. Arrival pauses the current colony until you explicitly choose SHIP DEPARTS.

Collected stock remains in colony storage until consumed or sold. The ship's Sell section collapses each resource to one top-level total, then shows quality bands underneath with their quantities, multipliers and individual unit prices. You can sell 25%, all of one band, or all colony stock.

Corporate purchases cost base sale price ×{{CORPORATE_BUY_MARKUP}} and enter the current colony immediately as Excellent grade. Imported resources can satisfy colony consumption and construction but do not count as local production toward contract production goals.

Stock Value on the HUD is the current quality-weighted sale value of all stored stock.

12. Contracts, goals & scoring

New mining charters begin as 10-year terms. To pass, all three objectives—Food Production, Industry capability and Population—must be at or above target when the deadline is checked.

Contract profit is local trade revenue − local costs. Passing all objectives earns Bronze. Silver additionally requires its profit threshold. Gold requires its higher profit threshold and every objective at 120%. Platinum requires the top threshold and every objective at 150%.

If the initial objectives are missed, up to {{MAX_EXTENSIONS}} one-year emergency extensions can be bought. Extension n costs £25,000 × n × contract progression tier. Exhausting the extensions terminates mining rights and creates a liability colony.

A successful first contract unlocks additional colonies. Completed colonies enter Holdover rather than ending the game. Renewals add {{RENEWAL_YEARS}} years at a time. Renewal cost starts around £100,000 × progression tier × world difficulty and rises by about ×2.1 for each previous renewal.

13. Multiple colonies & switching worlds

After your first successful contract, OPEN NEW COLONY offers new claims. Environmental difficulty and required Power/Food/Mining levels are checked before a colony can be established.

Each colony keeps a separate world seed/map, survey state, resource sites, inventory and quality bands, population, housing, Industry, contract clock, trade schedule and local financial performance. Corporation cash and technology remain shared.

Use COLONIES to open the portfolio grid and tap a colony to load it into the main screen. Inactive colonies do not freeze: they continue extraction, consumption, population simulation and operating costs while another colony is being viewed.

This makes portfolio planning important—an old colony can still fund the corporation, but it can also drain Food, Fuel, Ore and cash if ignored.

14. Returning colonies, refusal & relocation

After successful completion you may attempt to end/return a colony instead of renewing it. The corporation accepts a colony if it still has a viable renewable resource or at least {{RETURN_MIN_RESOURCE_RATIO_PCT}}% of its surveyed finite-resource base remaining.

If the world is too depleted, the corporation can refuse it. Ending the mining charter anyway turns it into a Liability Colony: surveying, new development and extraction stop, but the population remains and Food, Fuel, Ore and operating cash costs continue at the higher liability multiplier.

The permanent escape is population relocation. Cost is {{RELOCATION_BASE_COST}} + {{RELOCATION_PER_COLONIST}} per colonist + {{RELOCATION_PER_INDUSTRY_LEVEL}} per Industry level. You must have another colony before disposing of your final colony.

15. Saving, reset & diagnostics

The game saves to browser local storage. It saves periodically during play, when the app becomes hidden, and whenever important actions explicitly save. Use ☰ → Save now for a manual save.

Diagnostics shows build/save version, current colony, colony count, simulation heartbeat/ticks, scan queues and captured runtime errors. If a STARTUP ERROR or ERROR LOG badge appears, the diagnostics text is the best information to capture when reporting a problem.

HARD RESET ALL MINEIT DATA removes MineIT/Koplin browser saves and starts the corporation again from Contract 01. It affects the whole portfolio, not just the colony currently displayed.

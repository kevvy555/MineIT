# Stage 8 — Conglomerate vs Independent Startup Cost Comparison

This document establishes the current first-pass cost model for starting a conglomerate-backed colony operation versus an independently owned operation.

The purpose is to make the conglomerate's support explicit and to define what the player would eventually need to fund personally when moving toward independent operations.

> **Balancing note:** These figures are an initial economic calibration, not final balance values. The current target is for a minimum viable independent startup package to require roughly **£25–30 million** before meaningful physical colony development.

| Item | Purpose | Conglomerate Operation | Independent Operation |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **Player Colony Ship** | Transports colonists and initial cargo to establish the operation | **£0** — already player-owned | **£0** — already player-owned |
| **Planetary Scanner Suite** | Scans land and identifies potential resources | **£0** — corporate equipment | **£750k** |
| **Starter Technology Library** | Gives access to initial Housing, Power, Industry, Food and extraction technologies | **£0** — corporate licence | **£2.5m** |
| **Fabrication Design Library** | Provides the engineering designs/software needed to turn mined materials into buildings and equipment | **£0** — corporate licence | **£1.5m** |
| **Mining & Settlement Rights** | Legal authority to establish a colony and exploit planetary resources | **£0** — secured by conglomerate | **£5m** |
| **Navigation & Communications** | Long-range navigation data, communications and commercial connectivity | **£0** — corporate network | **£1m** |
| **Commercial Market Access** | Access to buyers, commodity markets and trading infrastructure | **£0** — corporate network | **£2m** |
| **Resource Certification System** | Certifies resource type, quality and purity so buyers will accept shipments | **£0** — corporate system | **£500k** |
| **Legal & Contract Infrastructure** | Handles contracts, compliance, payments and commercial administration | **£0** — corporate administration | **£750k** |
| **Specialist Startup Equipment** | Tools, diagnostics, replacement components and equipment not represented by bulk Build/Ore | **£0** — corporate startup package | **£1m** |
| **Freight Capability** | Provides a viable route for selling resources once production starts | **£0** — Corporate Ship/network initially available | **£6m minimum** — own/contract freight capability |
| **Emergency/Working Capital** | Allows the colony to survive breakdowns, delays and early operating losses | **Corporate-backed** | **£5m recommended reserve** |
| **Buildings & Infrastructure** | Housing, Power, Industry, extraction and other physical colony development | **Locally constructed** | **Locally constructed** |
| **Construction Materials** | Build/Ore and other physical resources used for colony development | **Locally mined** | **Locally mined** |

## Approximate Startup Requirement

### Conglomerate-backed colony

**£0 direct setup cost** to the player, excluding whatever supplies they voluntarily put aboard their ship.

In exchange, the conglomerate owns the contractual colony and infrastructure created under that arrangement.

### Independent colony

Approximately **£26 million** before meaningful physical colony development.

Indicative breakdown:

- £750k — Planetary scanner suite
- £2.5m — Starter technology library
- £1.5m — Fabrication design library
- £5m — Mining and settlement rights
- £1m — Navigation and communications
- £2m — Commercial market access
- £500k — Resource certification system
- £750k — Legal and contract infrastructure
- £1m — Specialist startup equipment
- £6m — Minimum freight capability
- £5m — Recommended working capital

## Core Distinction

The conglomerate-backed model is effectively:

> **You bring your ship, people and expertise. We provide the capability, rights, commercial access and support required to turn an undeveloped planet into a commercial mining operation.**

The player still mines the physical materials and constructs the colony themselves, but the conglomerate provides the expensive capability layer underneath that gameplay.

The independent model reverses that:

> **You fund the legal rights, technology, specialist equipment, commercial access and logistics yourself — but the resulting operation belongs to your company.**

## Design Principle

The physical colony gameplay should remain largely consistent between conglomerate-backed and independent colonies: the player still scans, mines resources and constructs infrastructure from local materials.

The key difference is ownership and the hidden support layer beneath those actions. Independence should therefore feel expensive because the player must replace capabilities the conglomerate previously supplied for free rather than because the game suddenly invents large quantities of externally supplied construction materials.

## Independence Design Direction

Avoid representing independence as a single **Pay £26m** action. Each major component should ultimately be acquired, owned, licensed, negotiated or established separately so that preparing for independence becomes a meaningful progression objective rather than simply a cash threshold.
